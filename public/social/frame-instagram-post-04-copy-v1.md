## Caption

Why does blood pressure matter?

Blood pressure is the force of blood pushing against artery walls as the heart pumps. It changes throughout the day, which means any one reading is only one moment.

Persistently high blood pressure often has no warning signs. Over time, it can affect the heart, brain, kidneys and eyes, and it increases the risk of heart disease and stroke.

That combination is important, often silent and measurable. It is why blood pressure is a core cardiovascular health metric.

Knowing your numbers matters, but measurement technique, repeated readings and clinical context matter too. Speak with a qualified health professional about what blood-pressure readings mean for you.
